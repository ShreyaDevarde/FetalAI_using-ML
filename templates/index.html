About the Project

What is FetalHealth?

This ML project analyses Cardiotocography (CTG) exam data a continuous electronic record of the baby's heart rate, to predict fetal wellbeing. In India, 20–30% of pregnancies are high-risk, responsible for 75% of perinatal morbidity and mortality.

CTG features are classified by expert obstetricians into three health states based on FIGO guidelines:

Normal
No hypoxia or acidosis — healthy fetal state
Suspect
Requires clinical evaluation and close monitoring
Pathological
Immediate medical intervention required
